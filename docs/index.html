About

I am completing the Master of Science in Information Technology at the University of the People. SecureSchool AI is my MSIT 5910 capstone. It applies software engineering, machine-learning evaluation, cybersecurity, and privacy design to a limited access-control problem.

The work is a research prototype, not a live school product. Minors are not enrolled. Raw photographs are not stored. Unauthorized results open a review item for a person.

  • Python · Flask · OpenCV (YuNet / SFace)
  • SQLite · encrypted embeddings · unit and system tests
  • Git / GitHub · layered + MVC architecture
  • Privacy-by-design · mixed quantitative and qualitative metrics

Featured project

Design and Evaluation of an Intelligent Facial Recognition Security Prototype for Controlled School Access

Role: Sole designer, developer, tester, and author · Course: MSIT 5910 · Instructor: Dr. Stella Tonye Whyte

The prototype enrolls a consenting adult under a research ID, captures one frame, matches an encrypted embedding, checks a named zone, and returns authorized, unauthorized, or review required. A matcher outage fails closed. Alerts do not lock a door or start a penalty.

Evaluation used the Unit 5 decision rules (match 0.363, review 0.28). Controlled trials and 11 automated tests verified the integrated path. Mean decision time stayed far below the two-second project target. Field photographs of volunteers remain a later, approved step.
